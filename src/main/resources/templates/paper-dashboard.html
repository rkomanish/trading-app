How Paper Trading Works
1. Market opens (09:15 IST) — StrategyEngine fires every minute.
2. Regime detection — ADX level determines which strategies are active.
3. Signal generated — Strategy sees a valid setup, creates a signal.
4. Sentiment veto — Claude checks RSS news headlines. If strongly bearish/bullish against the trade, it vetoes.
5. Risk check — RiskEngine validates: max daily loss, max lots, entry window, kill switch.
6. Paper fill — PaperBroker simulates fill at last DB candle price ± 0.02% slippage.
7. Square-off — All positions automatically closed at 15:15 IST.

To start paper trading: Make sure the app is running during market hours. The strategy engine runs on a cron schedule and will generate signals automatically. You can monitor everything on this page.
